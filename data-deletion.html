How to remove the data Widespread stores about you

You can delete everything Widespread holds about a connected account in any of these ways:

1. Disconnect inside Widespread

Open the group the account is connected to and disconnect Instagram or Facebook. Widespread immediately deletes the stored access token and the connection's credentials.

2. Remove Widespread from Facebook

In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations, find Widespread, and remove it. This revokes Widespread's access to your Facebook and Instagram accounts.

3. Request deletion by email

Email privacy@widespread.cc from the address associated with your account and ask for deletion. All stored tokens and credentials linked to your account will be removed.

See our Privacy Policy for what Widespread stores and why.
