Widespread

Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Widespread is a self-hosted tool that publishes your own short-form videos to the social accounts you connect — such as YouTube, Facebook Pages, and Instagram. It acts only on your behalf and only with accounts you explicitly link. This policy explains what it stores and how to remove it.

What Widespread stores

  • Access tokens you grant. When you connect a platform, the access token it issues is stored so Widespread can upload on your behalf and keep the connection alive. This includes Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google (YouTube) tokens.
  • Account identifiers. Basic identifiers for the accounts you connect — your Instagram Business account ID and username, your Facebook Page ID and name, the Meta app-scoped user ID of the login, and your YouTube channel — so uploads reach the right destination.
  • Video files and metadata you submit. The video, title, description, and tags you choose are used to perform the upload. Videos are processed transiently and are not kept after the upload completes beyond what the destination platform itself stores.
  • Your Widespread login. A username and a one-way hashed password (never stored in plain text) for signing in to Widespread itself.

How it is used

Stored data is used solely to publish and manage the content you ask Widespread to post. Widespread does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone other than the destination platforms you choose to connect.

Third-party platforms

Publishing necessarily sends your content and tokens to the platforms you connect. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies, including Meta and Google.

Retention and your choices

Tokens and connection details are kept until you disconnect the account or delete your data. You can revoke Widespread's access at any time from the platform's own settings (for Facebook and Instagram: Settings & privacy → Business integrations).

Deleting your data

You can remove everything Widespread has stored about you at any time. See Data deletion for the ways to do it.

Security

Data is stored on the server you run Widespread on and transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed; tokens are never exposed to other users or returned to the browser in full.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@widespread.cc.
